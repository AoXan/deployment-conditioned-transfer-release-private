# Data acquisition guide

Status **CONDITIONALLY_REPRODUCIBLE**. This release distributes source/configuration and aggregate evidence. It does not redistribute authorised farm inputs, private historical Checkpoints or APSIM binaries. Original experiment-file descriptions are the authority where independent metadata is unavailable; such claims are marked original-record provenance.

Hash method: downloader writes CRC and per-member SHA256 receipts; `verify_input_checksums.py` validates recorded publication checksums when exact inputs exist. For authorised new inputs record `shasum -a 256 FILE` alongside acquisition date, release version and licence; a new hash is an integrity receipt, not proof of equivalence to missing historical bytes. Do not invent expected hashes from current output. This audit downloaded actual public CY-Bench members and ran real preprocessing/training/diagnostics and APSIM; authorised Roseworthy, Waite and G2F full cases were not re-run.
